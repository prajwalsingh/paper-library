# 🗂️ Paper Library v2.1.0: See which collections a paper is in

> **Never lose track of where a paper is filed.** Click any paper, in **All References**, a search
> result, Recently Read or anywhere else, and Paper Library shows you its collections.

---

## ✨ What's new

### 📂 "In collections" at the top of the details panel
- Each collection the paper is in appears as a chip with its full path, e.g. `Neuroscience / Brain2Image`.
- **Filled chip** = the folder where the PDF file itself lives.
  **Outlined chip** = a collection it is *also* listed in (added with **Also add to…**).
  A one-line hint explains this whenever an outlined chip is shown.
- **Click a chip** to open that collection. The paper stays selected, so you see it in context.
- Not in any collection? You see **Unsorted: not in any collection**; click it to open Unsorted.
- Papers in Trash show **In Trash**.

### 🔦 The sidebar highlights the same collections
- The paper's collections get an **accent bar and accent text** in the sidebar.
- If one is inside a **collapsed** collection, the parent gets a lighter bar, so you know where to expand.
- Unsorted papers highlight **Unsorted** in the quick views.

Works the same on **Google Drive** and **OneDrive**.

---

## 🔧 Under the hood
- Only `library.html`, `library.css` and `library.js` changed. No new permissions and no change to the network lock.
- The collection chips used to sit at the bottom of the panel under *File*, where they were easy to miss.

## ⬆️ How to update
- **Cloned with git:** `git pull` in your Paper Library folder.
- **Downloaded the zip:** download **`paper-library-v2.1.0.zip`** below, check it, and unzip it over your existing folder:
  ```bash
  sha256sum -c paper-library-v2.1.0.zip.sha256
  ```

Then open `chrome://extensions` → **⟳** on Paper Library, and reopen any Paper Library tabs.
Your library, highlights and client IDs stay exactly as they are.

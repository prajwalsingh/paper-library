# 📝 Paper Library v1.3.0: Export your highlights

> **Your highlights, ready for your notes.** Turn everything you marked in a paper into a clean
> Markdown note with one click, for Obsidian, Notion, Logseq or your literature review.

---

## ✨ What's new

### 📤 Export highlights & notes as Markdown
Every highlight in reading order, grouped by page, with its colour and your note, under the
paper's title, authors, year and links.

```markdown
# Attention Is All You Need

**Authors:** Vaswani, Ashish; Shazeer, Noam  
**Year:** 2017  
**arXiv:** [1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

## Highlights (2, 1 with notes)

### Page 1

> 🟨 The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent …

**Note:** Key claim, compare with Mamba.
```

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| 📖 **Viewer** | **Menu → Export highlights & notes (Markdown)** downloads a `.md` file |
| 📋 **Viewer** | **Menu → Copy highlights & notes as Markdown** copies it for pasting anywhere |
| 📚 **Library** | Select a paper → **Export highlights** in the details panel (no need to open it) |

**Good to know**
- 🎨 Colours appear as 🟨 🟩 🟦 🩷 🟧 🟪, so you can still tell your colour code apart in plain text.
- 🔗 Highlights made in other apps (Evince, Okular, Adobe, Zotero) are exported too.
- ✂️ Words split across lines ("repre-sentation") are joined back together.
- 🔒 The file is made **in your browser** and saved by Chrome's normal download.
  Nothing is uploaded, and no new permission is needed.

---

## 🔧 Under the hood
- New shared module `js/export.js`, used by both the viewer and the library.
- The library reads highlights with PDF.js and fetches only the parts of the PDF it needs.
- `scripts/audit.py` lists `doi.org` and `arxiv.org` as **link-only** hosts. They appear as links
  inside exported notes and are never contacted. The network lock is unchanged.

## ⬆️ How to update
1. Download **`paper-library-v1.3.0.zip`** below and check it (optional but recommended):
   ```bash
   sha256sum -c paper-library-v1.3.0.zip.sha256
   ```
2. Unzip it **over your existing Paper Library folder** (same place you loaded it from).
3. Open `chrome://extensions` → click **⟳** on Paper Library. Close and reopen any open Paper Library tabs.

Your library, highlights and client ID stay exactly as they are. They live in Google Drive and
Chrome's storage, not in the folder.

      One-time setup: connect to Google Drive

      The extension needs its own Google “OAuth client” so it may read and update your Drive PDFs. Full step-by-step guide with troubleshooting: docs/SETUP.md.

      1. In Google Cloud Console, create a project and enable the Google Drive API.
      2. Configure the OAuth consent screen and add yourself as a test user (or choose “Internal” for a Workspace account).
